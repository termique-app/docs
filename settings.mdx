To open Settings, click your avatar in the top right and choose **Settings**.

Settings has these sections: **Account**, **Security**, **AI assistant**, **Appearance**, **Shortcuts**, **Subscription**, **Updates**, and **About**.

## Account

**Account** shows your name, email, and plan.

- **Invite friends**: your referral code with a **Copy** button, and how many people you invited and how many activated. Copying gives you a shareable link. You and the person you invite both get a reward once they connect to a host.
- **Lock**: locks the vault and keeps local data in place.
- **Sign out**: disconnects live sessions and clears this device's local database: hosts, groups, snippets, and settings. Your account on the server is untouched, so signing back in downloads it all again. If Touch ID is on, signing out also removes it for this account.

<Warning>
  **Delete account** permanently deletes your hosts and credentials, groups, snippets, command audit logs, sent and received share invites, AI memories and settings, your profile, and your sign-in account. This cannot be undone. Click **Delete…**, type your email to confirm, then click **Delete account**.
</Warning>

## Security

| Setting | What it does |
|---------|--------------|
| **Touch ID** | Unlock with your fingerprint instead of the master password. You confirm your master password to turn it on. macOS only. |
| **Require password on startup** | On by default. Turn it off only on a personal device you trust. Termique then unlocks from your OS keychain at launch. |
| **Auto-lock after** | Locks the app after this much inactivity: `5 minutes`, `15 minutes`, `30 minutes`, `1 hour`, or `2 hours`. Default `30 minutes`. |
| **Log commands on new hosts** | Turns on command logging for hosts you create from now on. Existing hosts keep their own setting. |
| **Lock after 5 wrong passwords** | Always on. After 5 wrong master passwords, the app locks for 5 minutes. |

To change your master password, fill in **Current password**, **New password**, and **Confirm new password** under **Master password**, then click **Change password**. The new password must be at least `8` characters.

See [Security and encryption](/security) for how the master password protects your credentials.

## AI assistant

Choose the provider for the AI assistant:

- **Hosted**: runs on the Termique API. `15` prompts a day on Free, `100` on Pro.
- **Custom endpoint**: any OpenAI-compatible API. Enter the **API base URL**, **Model**, and **API key**. The key is stored in your OS keychain. Turn on **Send terminal output to this endpoint** to allow terminal output you attach to a message to be sent there.

Click **Save** to apply. See [AI assistant](/ai-assistant).

## Appearance

Pick **Light**, **Dark**, or **Match system**. **Match system** follows your OS setting and is the default. You can also switch between light and dark from the account menu.

Under **Terminal**, **Copy on select** copies text when you select it with the mouse. Right-click pastes. This setting applies to this computer only.

## Shortcuts

**Shortcuts** lists keyboard shortcuts in groups: **Global**, **Sessions**, **Switch to tab**, **Panes**, and **Terminal**.

Click a shortcut to record a new combination. Press `Esc` to cancel recording. If the combination is already used by another action, Termique shows **Shortcut conflict**. Click **Override** to move it, or **Cancel**.

Click **Reset to defaults** to restore every shortcut.

## Subscription

**Subscription** shows your plan and its status, plus usage tiles for **Share recipients** and **AI prompts today**. See [Plans and billing](/plans) to upgrade or manage billing.

## Updates

**Updates** shows your installed version and checks for a new one when you open it.

1. Click **Check for updates**, or **Check again** after a previous check.
2. If an update is available, review the release notes and click **Download update**.
3. When the download finishes, click **Restart & install**.

Termique also checks for updates shortly after launch. If one is available, a notification offers **Install & restart**.

<Note>
  On macOS, installing an update asks for your system password. Enter it and keep Termique open until the update finishes.
</Note>

## About

**About** shows your version and links to the **Website**, **Changelog**, and **Report an issue**.
