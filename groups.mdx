---
title: "Groups"
description: "Organize hosts into named, color-coded groups and connect to all of them at once."
---

Groups are named collections of hosts. Use them to separate environments (e.g. `production`, `staging`), projects, or regions. A host can belong to more than one group.

Groups appear as folders in the sidebar under **Hosts**, sorted by name. A host in several groups is listed under each one. Hosts in no group are listed after the folders.

## Open the groups list

Click a folder in the sidebar to open that group, then click **Groups** in the page header to see every group. You can also open the command palette (`⌘K` or `Ctrl+K`) and choose **Groups**.

Each row shows the group's color, the first hosts in it, the host count, and how many hosts are online. A host counts as online when it has a connected session or a reporting monitoring agent. Use **Filter groups** to search by name.

## Create a group

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open New group">
    Click the **New folder** button next to **Hosts** in the sidebar, or click **New group** on the groups list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it">
    Enter a **Name** and pick a **Color**: Blue, Green, Yellow, Purple, Orange, or Pink.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Create group**. A new group starts empty.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Add hosts to a group

Open the group and click **Add hosts**. Select hosts from the list (search by label, host, or username) and click **Add 2 hosts** (the count matches your selection). Hosts already in the group are not listed. If every host is already in the group, the dialog offers **New host**.

You can also assign groups from a host's **Add host** or **Edit host** sheet. Use the **Groups** field to add the host to one or more groups.

## Group detail

The group page lists its hosts with the same status, address, CPU, memory, and last-connected columns as the hosts list. Click a host to open its detail page, or hover a row and click **Connect**.

The side panel shows:

- **Colour**: click a swatch to change the group's color.
- **Summary**: **Hosts**, **Online**, **With agent**, and **Last seen**.
- **Rename** and **Delete group, keep hosts**.

## Connect to every host in a group

Click **Open all** in the group header to open one terminal session per host. If the group has more than 3 hosts, Termique asks you to confirm first. For a group with one host the button reads **Connect**.

## Remove a host from a group

On the group page, right-click the host and select **Remove from group**, then confirm. The host is not deleted.

The same context menu has **Connect**, **Details**, and **Edit host**. **Edit host** is not shown for hosts shared with you.

## Rename or delete a group

On the group page, click **Rename** to change its name, color, and members in the **Edit group** dialog. On the groups list, hover a row and click the pencil icon to do the same.

To delete a group, click **Delete group, keep hosts** on the group page, or the trash icon on the groups list. The hosts in it are not deleted.

## Sync

Groups and their host lists sync through the API to your other devices. When you add or remove a host on one device, the change appears on your other devices on the next sync.
