Termique is a desktop SSH manager for engineers who work across many hosts. It combines a terminal with saved hosts, encrypted credential storage, and sync across your devices.

Your credentials are encrypted on your device with a key protected by your master password. The master password never leaves your device.

Termique also includes port forwarding, incident investigations, and a [mobile companion app](/mobile).

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Termique opens your browser to sign in. Your account identifies you and enables sync. It never has access to your credentials.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a master password">
    On first launch, you choose a master password. Termique derives a key from it and uses that key to wrap a random Data Encryption Key (DEK). The DEK encrypts every credential. There is no way to recover a lost master password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add hosts">
    Save a hostname, port, username, and a password or SSH key. The credential is encrypted with the DEK before it is stored.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect">
    Open a session from the host list or the host's page. Termique decrypts the credential locally and opens the SSH connection.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sync">
    Hosts, groups, snippets, SSH keys, and port forwards sync to your other signed-in devices. The sync backend only receives encrypted credentials.
  </Step>
</Steps>
