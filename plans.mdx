Termique has a Free plan and a Pro subscription. Everyone starts on Free. Pro removes the host and snippet caps, raises AI limits, and adds sharing, command logs, and longer metrics history.

## Free vs Pro

| Feature | Free | Pro |
|---------|------|-----|
| Synced hosts | Up to `3` | Unlimited |
| Snippets | Up to `10` | Unlimited |
| SFTP file transfer | Included | Included |
| Port forwarding | Included | Included |
| Host monitoring | Included | Included |
| Hosted AI assistant | `15` prompts per day | `100` prompts per day |
| Hosted inline AI suggestions | `30` per day | `200` per day |
| AI memory | Not included | Included |
| Host sharing and command logs | Not included | Included |
| Incident investigations | Not included | Included |
| Background alert notifications | Not included | Included |
| Metrics history | See [Metrics history](#metrics-history) | See [Metrics history](#metrics-history) |

Hosted AI limits reset at `00:00` UTC.

<Note>
  A custom AI endpoint is not counted against daily limits on either plan. See [AI assistant](/ai-assistant).
</Note>

### Share recipients

Pro includes `5` share recipients. A recipient is one person you share with, however many hosts you share with them. Additional recipients cost `$3`/month each and are added in the [web dashboard](https://termique.app/dashboard).

### Metrics history

| Resolution | Free | Pro |
|------------|------|-----|
| Raw | 24 hours | 24 hours |
| 5 minutes | 7 days | 30 days |
| 1 hour | 30 days | 1 year |
| 1 day | 90 days | 3 years |

The **1y** range on monitoring charts requires Pro.

## Upgrade

1. Open **Settings → Subscription** and click **Upgrade to Pro**.
2. Review the Free and Pro comparison and click **Upgrade to Pro**.
3. Checkout opens in your browser on the web dashboard. Payment is handled by Polar.

You can also upgrade from the last onboarding step, or from any upgrade prompt in the app.

## Manage your subscription

Open **Settings → Subscription** and click **Manage billing**. The billing section of the web dashboard opens in your browser, where you can update payment details, view invoices, or cancel.

**Subscription** shows your plan status:

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Active** | Pro is active. The renewal date is shown next to it. |
| **Canceling** | You canceled. Pro stays active until the date shown. |
| **Payment issue** | A payment failed. Pro features are off until it is resolved. New shares are paused, and existing shares keep working. |
| **Granted** | Pro was granted to your account, for example as a referral reward. There is nothing to pay or manage. An end date is shown if it has one. |

## Hitting a limit

On Free, adding an 11th snippet, or a 4th host when your hosts all sync, opens an upgrade prompt instead of completing the action. Termique does not delete anything. Remove an existing item or upgrade to continue.

### Hosts on this computer

When your Free account supports hosts kept on this computer, the **Hosts** page shows a **Sync** counter such as **Sync 3/3**. You can then add as many hosts as you like:

- `3` hosts sync to your other devices.
- The rest stay on this computer only. Their address, credential, and port forwards never leave the device.
- To change which hosts sync, click the **Sync** counter, or right-click a host and choose **Sync to other devices** or **Swap with a local host**.

When you upgrade to Pro, every host on this computer syncs.

## Devices

Your account can be signed in on a limited number of devices. If a new device is over the limit, Termique shows **This device could not be registered**. Click **Manage devices** to remove a device in the web dashboard. Removing a device from the list does not sign it out.
