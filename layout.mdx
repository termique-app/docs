After you unlock Termique, you land on the **Hosts** page. The window has three parts: the sidebar on the left, a thin top bar, and the content panel.

## Sidebar

The sidebar holds your open sessions, your hosts, and the tools. From top to bottom:

- **Search**: opens the command palette.
- **All hosts**: opens the [Hosts](/hosts) page. The number is your host count.
- **Sessions**: your open terminal sessions and investigations. Click **+** to open a local shell. Hover a session and click the close button to end it.
- **Hosts**: your groups shown as folders, followed by hosts that are in no group. Click a folder to open the [group](/groups), or click a host to open its page. Use the buttons next to the heading to add a **New folder** or a **New host**.
- **Tools**: **Snippets**, **SSH keys**, **Port forwards**, **Monitoring**, **Command logs**, **Investigations**, and **Teams**. On Free, **Command logs** carries a **Pro** badge and opens an upgrade prompt.

Each host shows a status dot:

| Dot | Meaning |
|-----|---------|
| Green | Connected |
| Gray | Not connected |
| Amber | Monitoring agent offline |

A link icon marks a host shared with you. A monitor icon marks a host kept only on this computer.

To collapse the sidebar to an icon rail, click **Hide sidebar** next to the logo. Click **Show sidebar** to expand it again.

## Top bar

The top bar shows:

- **Sync status**: whether your data is synced. If sync fails, click **Retry**.
- **Notifications**: pending share invites. A red dot means an invite is waiting. Accept or decline it from the panel.
- **Account menu**: your avatar. It shows your plan (**Free** or **Pro**) and holds a light and dark **Theme** switch, **Settings**, **About**, **Report bug**, **Lock screen**, and **Sign out**.

<Tip>
  To step away, use **Lock screen**. It locks the vault and keeps your local data. **Sign out** disconnects live sessions and clears this device's local database.
</Tip>

## Command palette

Press `⌘K` on macOS or `Ctrl+K` on Windows and Linux, or click **Search** in the sidebar. The palette searches hosts, sessions, pages, actions, and keyboard shortcuts. Select a host to connect to it.

## Web dashboard

Billing, devices, and extra share recipients are managed in the web dashboard at [termique.app/dashboard](https://termique.app/dashboard), not in the desktop app. Links such as **Manage billing** and **Manage devices** open it in your browser. See [Plans and billing](/plans).
