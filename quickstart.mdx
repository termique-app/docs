This guide covers installing Termique, setting up your master password, and opening your first SSH session.

## Prerequisites

- A computer running macOS, Windows, or Linux
- An SSH server you can reach: hostname or IP, port, username, and a password or private key

## Set up Termique

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download the app">
    Download the latest release for your platform from [termique.app/download](https://termique.app/download) and install it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Open Termique and click **Sign in with browser**. Your default browser opens to complete sign-in. When it finishes, return to the app.

    To stop waiting for the browser, click **Cancel**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/login.png" alt="Sign-in screen with the Sign in with browser button" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete onboarding">
    On first sign-in, a 4-step setup runs:

    1. **Welcome**: click **Get started**.
    2. **Create master password**: enter a master password of at least `8` characters and confirm it. Check **I understand my master password cannot be recovered if lost.**, then click **Set master password**.
    3. **Choose your theme**: pick **Light**, **Dark**, or **System**, then click **Continue**.
    4. **Compare Free and Pro**: click **Continue to app**, or **Upgrade to Pro** to open checkout in your browser.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/master-password-setup.png" alt="Create master password step with password strength meter and recovery acknowledgement" />
    </Frame>

    <Warning>
      There is no master password recovery. If you lose it, your stored credentials cannot be decrypted. Store it in a password manager.
    </Warning>

    <Note>
      If you already use Termique on another device, onboarding is skipped. Enter your existing master password on the unlock screen instead.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your first host">
    After onboarding, you land on the **Hosts** page. Click **New host**. You can also click **+** next to **Hosts** in the sidebar, or press `⌘N` on macOS or `Ctrl+Shift+N` on Windows and Linux.

    In the **Add host** panel, fill in:

    - **Label**: a name for the host, such as `web-prod-01`
    - **Host or IP**: the hostname or IP address. You can also enter `host:port`.
    - **Port**: the SSH port. Default `22`.
    - **Username**: the SSH user
    - **Authentication**: **Password**, **Stored SSH key**, or **Private key**

    **Groups**, **Tags**, and **Connect via** (a jump host) are optional.

    Click **Add host**. Termique encrypts the credential and opens the host's page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect">
    On the host's page, click **Open session**. From the **Hosts** list, hover a row and click **Connect**, or select it and press `Enter`.

    The session appears under **Sessions** in the sidebar. Click it to return to the terminal.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/terminal.png" alt="Terminal session with the session listed under Sessions in the sidebar" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>
